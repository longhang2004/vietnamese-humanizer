# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Vietnamese writers using ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or another chatbot, including people who do not write code. Developers can install the same skills in their agents.

## Product Purpose

Help people review Vietnamese grammar, translated phrasing, consistency and writing style while retaining facts and their own voice.

## Operating Context

The website offers a rule-based checker and downloads/prompts for chatbots. AI rewriting and corpus contributions follow backend capability flags. The landing page and guides work without the backend.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Four skills, 44 rule patterns, six specialized writing profiles plus preservation, 103 corpus examples and 124 benchmark cases at v0.6.0. There is no independently human-reviewed quality baseline. The ChatGPT plugin is packaged but has no public listing. GitHub is the canonical repository; the site is deployed on Vercel.

## Brand Commitments

Vietnamese Writing Skills. Direct, natural Vietnamese UI copy. The user explicitly chose a dark interface oriented toward technical users on 04-11-2026. Preserve a usable path for nontechnical readers.

## Evidence on Hand

Six real pairs generated by separate Codex subagents are recorded in `docs/research/2026-11-04-web-comparisons/`. Both conditions receive identical fictional drafts and editing requests. Published outputs remain verbatim, with provenance; they are illustrations rather than quality scores.

## Product Principles

- Keep the original meaning and authorial voice.
- Show the actual product and evidence before asking people to install.
- Make the next action and its result clear.
- Preserve working, accessible controls and fail-closed optional capabilities.
